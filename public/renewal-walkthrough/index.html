How renewals work, start to finish

One subscription, followed through every stage. On the left is what your system does. On the right is what your customer sees. Everything here runs for real in test mode.

1

Your customer signs up

Every payment method for their country is offered. What they pick decides how renewals work.

Your system

You put a managed subscription product in checkout. Nothing else changes in your integration.

A new email each time gives a brand new customer account.
Pays by card Card is saved. Renewals charge it automatically.
Pays by invoice or wire Nothing is saved. Each renewal sends them an invoice to pay.
Your customer
