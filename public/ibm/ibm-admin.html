Manage IBM-sub subscribers

Usage-based Managed Subscription - real usage isn't tracked in this demo, so charges (manual or via the batch job below) are always a deliberate amount you or the simulator picks, against FastSpring's Managed Subscription API.

Monthly rebill

IBM-sub charges on the 1st of every month for the prior month's usage. Since there's no real usage metering here, each run charges every active subscriber a random amount - mostly small, with a deliberate slice of high amounts so that edge case gets exercised too, not just the common case.
